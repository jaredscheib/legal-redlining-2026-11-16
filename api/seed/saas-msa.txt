SaaS Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made between the provider of the hosted
software service identified in the Order Form ("Provider") and the customer identified in the
Order Form ("Customer"). This Agreement governs Customer's access to and use of the Service.

1. Definitions.
1.1 "Service" means the hosted software platform, including updates, made available by Provider
as described in the Documentation and any Order Form.
1.2 "Order Form" means an ordering document executed by both parties that references this
Agreement and specifies the subscription term, fees and usage limits.
1.3 "Customer Data" means all data and content submitted to the Service by or for Customer.
1.4 "Proprietary Information" means any business or technical information of a party that is
marked or identified as proprietary or that by its nature should reasonably be treated as such.

2. Provision of the Service.
2.1 Subject to this Agreement and timely payment of fees, Provider grants Customer a
non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the
Service for its internal business purposes.
2.2 Customer shall not (a) sublicense, resell or make the Service available to any third party;
(b) reverse engineer the Service; or (c) use the Service to build a competing product.

3. Service Levels.
3.1 Provider shall make the Service available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month,
excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.
3.2 If Provider fails to meet the service level in any month, Customer is entitled to a service
credit equal to ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each full one percent (1%) below the
target, up to fifty percent (50%) of the monthly fees. Service credits are Customer's sole and
exclusive remedy for unavailability.

4. Fees and Payment.
4.1 Customer shall pay the fees set out in each Order Form. Fees are invoiced annually in
advance and are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
4.2 Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month
or the maximum rate permitted by law.
4.3 Fees exclude taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use and similar taxes, other
than taxes on Provider's net income.

5. Customer Data and Security.
5.1 Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. Provider shall use Customer Data only to
provide and improve the Service and as Customer instructs.
5.2 Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to
protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, consistent with industry standards.
5.3 Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72)
hours, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data.

6. Proprietary Information.
6.1 Each party shall protect the other party's Proprietary Information with reasonable care,
use it only to perform under this Agreement and not disclose it except to personnel and
contractors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of non-disclosure.
6.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the
receiving party or that was independently developed.

Documentation. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is for Provider to

7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER
DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

8. Indemnification.
8.1 Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold Customer harmless from and against any third-party
claim alleging that the Service infringes any patent, copyright or trademark, and shall pay any
damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
8.2 Customer shall indemnify Provider against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data
or Customer's use of the Service in violation of this Agreement.
8.3 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, reasonable cooperation and sole
control of the defense to the indemnifying party.

9.1 EXCEPT FOR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS AND BREACHES OF SECTION 6, NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL

10. Term, Renewal and Cancellation.
10.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have
expired or been cancelled.
10.2 Each Order Form renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party
gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
10.3 Customer may cancel this Agreement at any time, for any reason, upon thirty (30) days'
prior written notice to Provider, provided that prepaid fees are non-refundable except as
stated in Section 7.1.
10.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured
within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.
10.5 Upon expiration or cancellation, Customer's right to use the Service ends, and Provider
shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days and then delete it.
