Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into between the corporation identified
on the signature page (the "Company") and the individual identified on the signature page
("Employee").

1. Position and Duties.
1.1 The Company employs Employee in the position of Senior Product Manager, reporting to the
Vice President of Product. Employee shall perform the duties customarily associated with that
position and such other duties as the Company may reasonably assign.
1.2 Employee shall devote substantially all of Employee's business time and efforts to the
Company and shall not engage in any other employment without the Company's written consent.

2. Start Date and At-Will Employment.
2.1 Employment begins on the Start Date set out on the signature page.
2.2 Employment is at will. Either the Company or Employee may end the employment relationship at
any time, with or without Cause, subject to the notice and payment terms of Section 7.

3. Compensation.
3.1 The Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary, payable in accordance with the
Company's regular payroll practices and subject to applicable withholding.
3.2 Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of fifteen percent (15%)
of base salary, determined by the Company in its sole discretion.
3.3 Subject to approval by the board of directors, Employee will be granted an option to
purchase shares of the Company's common stock, vesting over four (4) years with a one-year cliff.

4. Benefits and Expenses.
4.1 Employee may participate in the employee benefit plans the Company makes available to
similarly situated employees, subject to the terms of those plans.
4.2 Employee is entitled to twenty (20) days of paid time off each calendar year.
4.3 The Company shall reimburse reasonable business expenses incurred in accordance with Company
policy.

5. Proprietary Information.
5.1 Employee shall not, during or after employment, use or disclose any Proprietary Information
of the Company except as required to perform Employee's duties. "Proprietary Information"
includes trade secrets, inventions, product roadmaps, customer and supplier lists, pricing,
personnel information and any other non-public information relating to the Company's business.
5.2 On separation, Employee shall return all Company property and all documents containing
Proprietary Information.
5.3 Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from reporting possible violations of law to a
government agency or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws.

6. Inventions.
6.1 Employee assigns to the Company all inventions, works of authorship and improvements that
Employee conceives during employment that relate to the Company's business or result from work
performed for the Company, other than inventions excluded by applicable law.

7. Separation.
7.1 The Company may dismiss Employee for Cause immediately upon written notice. "Cause" means
Employee's (a) material breach of this Agreement; (b) willful misconduct; (c) conviction of a
felony; or (d) repeated failure to perform assigned duties after written warning.
7.2 If the Company dismisses Employee other than for Cause, the Company shall pay Employee
separation pay equal to three (3) months of base salary, conditioned on Employee signing and not
revoking a general release of claims.
7.3 Employee may resign upon at least two (2) weeks' written notice to the Company.
7.4 Upon any separation, the Company shall pay all earned but unpaid salary and accrued, unused
paid time off.

8. Non-Solicitation.
8.1 During employment and for twelve (12) months after the employment ends for any reason,
Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any employee of the Company to leave the
Company, or solicit any customer of the Company with whom Employee had material contact to
reduce its business with the Company.

9. Return of Property.
9.1 Employee shall return all laptops, badges, keys and other Company equipment on the last day
of employment.

10. Dispute Resolution.
10.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by final and binding arbitration
administered under the employment arbitration rules of a nationally recognized provider, before a
single arbitrator, in the county where Employee primarily works.
10.2 Either party may seek temporary injunctive relief in court to protect Proprietary
Information pending arbitration.

11. Governing Law.
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Employee primarily works.

12. Entire Agreement.
12.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter
and supersedes any offer letter or prior understanding. It may be modified only in a writing
signed by Employee and an authorized officer of the Company.
12.2 If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in
full force, and the unenforceable provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to
make it enforceable.
