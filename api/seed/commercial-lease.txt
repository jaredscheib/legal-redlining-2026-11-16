Commercial Lease Agreement

This Commercial Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is made between the owner of the Building
("Landlord") and the business entity identified in the Basic Lease Information ("Tenant").

1. Premises.
1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the space described in Exhibit A
(the "Premises") on the third floor of the office building located at the address stated in the
Basic Lease Information (the "Building"), together with the non-exclusive right to use the
common areas.

2. Term.
2.1 The term of this Lease begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the last day of the
sixtieth (60th) full calendar month thereafter (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner ended as
provided in this Lease.
2.2 Tenant has one option to extend the term for five (5) years on the same terms, except that
Base Rent shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the then fair market rent. Tenant shall exercise
the option by written notice given at least nine (9) months before the Expiration Date.

3. Rent.
3.1 Tenant shall pay Base Rent in the monthly amounts stated in the Basic Lease Information, in
advance, on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction or set-off.
3.2 Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the Commencement Date.
3.3 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, its proportionate share of Operating Expenses and Real
Estate Taxes in excess of those incurred in the base year.
3.4 If any installment of rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant
shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount.

4. Security Deposit.
4.1 On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord a security deposit equal to two (2)
months of Base Rent. Landlord may apply the security deposit to cure any default by Tenant.
4.2 Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the security deposit to Tenant within thirty
(30) days after the later of the Expiration Date or the date Tenant surrenders the Premises.

5. Use.
5.1 Tenant shall use the Premises only for general office purposes and for no other purpose
without Landlord's prior written consent.
5.2 Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its use of the Premises and with the
Building rules adopted by Landlord from time to time.

6. Maintenance and Repairs.
6.1 Landlord shall maintain the roof, structure, common areas and base building systems in good
order and repair.
6.2 Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition, reasonable wear and tear
excepted, and shall promptly repair any damage caused by Tenant or its invitees.

7. Alterations.
7.1 Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises costing more than ten thousand dollars
without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

8. Insurance.
8.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least two
million dollars per occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.
8.2 Each party waives claims against the other for property damage to the extent covered by
insurance it carries or is required to carry.

9. Hold Harmless.
9.1 Tenant shall defend and hold harmless Landlord and its agents from all claims, losses and
expenses arising from any injury to persons or damage to property occurring in the Premises,
except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord.
9.2 Landlord shall hold Tenant harmless from claims arising in the common areas, except to the
extent caused by Tenant's negligence.

10. Assignment and Subletting.
10.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's
prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

11. Early Exit Right.
11.1 Provided Tenant is not in default, Tenant may end this Lease effective as of the last day of
the thirty-sixth (36th) month of the term by giving Landlord at least six (6) months' written
notice and paying an exit fee equal to three (3) months of then-current Base Rent plus the
unamortized cost of Landlord's improvements.

12. Default and Remedies.
12.1 Each of the following is an event of default: (a) Tenant fails to pay rent within five (5)
days after notice that it is due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty
(30) days after notice; or (c) Tenant becomes insolvent or abandons the Premises.
12.2 Upon an event of default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice, re-enter the
Premises as permitted by law and recover all damages, including unpaid rent for the remainder of
the term, discounted to present value, less the fair rental value of the Premises.

13. Surrender.
13.1 At the end of the term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good
condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted, and remove its personal property.
13.2 If Tenant holds over without consent, Tenant shall pay rent at one hundred fifty percent
(150%) of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the term.

14. Governing Law.
14.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of the state in which the Building is located.

15. Miscellaneous.
15.1 This Lease is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Premises.
15.2 Time is of the essence of each provision of this Lease.
15.3 Any notice under this Lease must be in writing and delivered to the addresses stated in the
Basic Lease Information.
